Hi!



I am excited to announce a new initiative called Marathon that encourages you to work with fellow AI GDEs as a team for a longer period.

Almost all of you participated in Sprints, which is a single-person project over a short period, so this Marathon concept should be quite familiar and intuitive to you. :-) 



Our first Marathon destination is JAX on TPU Tutorial! 

It would be really cool to create a series of Colab notebooks covering a variety of topics of JAX on TPU like https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials or  https://keras.io/guides/ .

It will be helpful either for us to teach specific topics to developers or for them to learn independently.



Here are a few details:

- Output format: End-to-end examples with deep dives into specific topics for JAX with Colab notebook(s) using TPU backend 

- Recommended license of the content of the notebook: Apache 2.0 (We may consider creating a GitHub repo later hence having the same Open Source license is needed.)

- Maximum number of people to collaborate with for a notebook: 3 (It is perfectly fine to work solo as well!)

- Deadline to complete the notebook: Jan 31, 2027

- We will prepare cool swag(s) for those of you who complete the project


Interested? Then feel free to sign up here!
